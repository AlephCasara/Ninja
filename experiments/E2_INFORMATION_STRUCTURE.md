# E2 — Information Structure

**Status:** design only. Requires usable E1 data.

## Question

Does the *structure* of public discussion provide incremental information beyond attention intensity?

## Feature families

### Divergence

- mean stance;
- variance;
- polarization;
- cross-platform disagreement.

### Semantic structure

- semantic homogeneity;
- topic entropy;
- narrative concentration;
- novelty;
- emergence/change points.

## Core ablation

```text
market only
market + attention
market + attention + stance
market + attention + disagreement
market + attention + narrative structure
market + all validated E2 components
```

## Requirement

A frozen E2 candidate must demonstrate incremental value over the corresponding simpler attention-only baseline on replication evidence not used to invent that candidate.

A negative result rejects only that scoped representation, population, target and horizon. It does not reject semantic or narrative structure as a research family.
