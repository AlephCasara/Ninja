# Business Domain

Business is a future Ninja domain, not a fork and not a current implementation priority.

It may reuse validated domain-neutral methods from Ninja Labs, but it must define and validate its own:

- commercial targets;
- commercial baselines;
- commercial susceptibility/context;
- opportunity definitions;
- promotion criteria;
- Business Master integration.

Trading evidence does not validate business hypotheses automatically.

During the trading historical-validation program this directory remains intentionally minimal. New business code should not delay E1 or the shared contracts needed for E1/E3.
